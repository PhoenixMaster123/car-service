Who We Are

More Than Just a Garage

Founded in 2015, Haris Car Care began with a simple mission: to provide a level of detailing and mechanical care that didn't exist in the market. What started as a single-bay garage has grown into the region's premier auto care center.

We combine state-of-the-art technology with old-school craftsmanship. Whether it's a routine oil change or a full ceramic coating, we treat every vehicle as if it were our own.
